VERY IMPORTANT NOTE: On the 'HELP!' album 'Yesterday'
is played in G on guitar, but with the full tone
dropped tuning the song then sounds in the key of F

Alternatively, here's a rare live band version video
without any guitar detuning, played in the key of G
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4YWyFIzSeXI
